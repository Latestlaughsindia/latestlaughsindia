Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers "Latest Laughs Poster", an internal automation used only by Latest Laughs India to publish videos to accounts we own.

Who uses it

Only the owner of Latest Laughs India. No other people sign in, and the tool is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to our own channel.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets: stores our own video files and a tracking spreadsheet in our own account.
  • Instagram and Facebook: publishes Reels to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any viewer or other user. Public YouTube information, such as video titles, view counts and public comments, is read only to choose which moments to clip.

Google API data

Latest Laughs Poster's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
